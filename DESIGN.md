# Design System: The Pharma Coach

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Clinical Signal"**

The system combines the structure of a pharmaceutical field guide with the momentum of a sales-training workshop. Public Sans, strong ruled layouts, dark authority fields, and decisive blue surfaces carry the identity. Signal Lime appears only at the exact moment attention or action matters.

The system rejects generic hospital teal, editorial luxury, artificial imagery, rounded software cards, broad neon backgrounds, and unsubstantiated hype.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Structured, confident grids
- High-contrast ink and clinical white
- Method Blue as the primary action color
- Limited neon signals
- Authentic people and real work environments

## Colors

The palette comes from Jebb's approved slide deck and is adapted for accessible web use.

### Primary

- **Method Blue** (`#1552F0`): Primary buttons, links, full-color brand bands, and selected words.
- **Pharma Ink** (`#08090C`): Header, footer, authority sections, and primary text.

### Secondary

- **Signal Lime** (`#B6FF00`): Small labels, arrows, icons, active states, and short emphasis on dark surfaces. Keep it below roughly 8% of a page.

### Neutral

- **Clinical White** (`#F7F9FC`): Main page canvas and light text on dark surfaces.
- **Cool Slate** (`#545B68`): Supporting copy and metadata.
- **Pale Mist** (`#EAF0F5`): Section contrast and quiet depth.
- **Rule Line** (`#CBD5DF`): Borders, dividers, and structural rules.

**The Neon Rule:** Signal with lime; do not decorate with it. Never use it for paragraphs or large light-background fields.

## Typography

Public Sans Variable is the sole brand family. It gives the site pharmaceutical precision without losing the warmth required for a coaching relationship.

- Display: 700 weight with tight tracking and compact line height.
- Lead copy: 500 weight with slightly tightened tracking.
- Body: 400 weight, approximately 65 to 75 characters per line.
- Labels and controls: 800 weight with uppercase tracking.

Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and spacing rather than a decorative second family.

## Elevation

The system is primarily flat and structural. Use thin rules, solid color fields, and occasional hard offset shadows for campaign graphics. Avoid ambient card shadows, translucent glass, and soft floating panels.

## Components

- **Primary button:** Method Blue field, Clinical White label, Signal Lime arrow or micro-accent. Square corners and direct language.
- **Dark button:** Pharma Ink field with Clinical White text for secondary dark actions.
- **Section label:** Method Blue on light surfaces; Signal Lime on dark surfaces.
- **Cards:** Use borders and tonal contrast. Avoid unnecessary rounding and nested cards.
- **Photography:** Documentary, naturally lit, and specific to real work. Do not over-retouch faces.
- **Focus:** Use a visible Signal Lime or Method Blue focus outline with sufficient offset.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Let Method Blue carry large brand moments.
- Use Signal Lime for one decisive point at a time.
- Keep body copy in Pharma Ink or Cool Slate.
- Pair strong color fields with generous clinical-white space.
- Preserve Public Sans across website, slides, and supporting materials.

### Don't

- Reintroduce orange as a primary brand color.
- Place lime paragraph text on white or mist backgrounds.
- Fill whole sections with neon.
- Add decorative gradients or glass panels.
- Use synthetic portraits or generic medical stock imagery.
