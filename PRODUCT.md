# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Ambitious professionals evaluating pharmaceutical sales as their next career move. The core audiences are nurses and healthcare workers with clinical credibility, experienced sales professionals, educators who can explain complex ideas, and pharmaceutical representatives pursuing advancement.

## Product Purpose

The Pharma Coach website helps qualified career changers understand how their existing experience translates into pharmaceutical sales, assess whether the field fits them, and choose an appropriate coaching path. Success means visitors see a credible route forward and take the next deliberate step without inflated promises.

## Brand Personality

Credible, clinical, energized. The brand should feel like an experienced pharmaceutical hiring manager who communicates clearly, respects the visitor's background, and brings focused momentum to a high-stakes career decision.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic hospital teal, luxury-editorial styling, synthetic corporate portraits, motivational-speaker hype, saturated neon fields, rounded SaaS card systems, and claims that imply guaranteed hiring or earnings.

## Design Principles

1. Make expertise visible through specificity, structure, and proof.
2. Translate clinical and professional experience instead of treating visitors like beginners.
3. Use energy as a signal, not as decoration.
4. Keep decisions clear: one primary action and an honest next step.
5. Prefer authentic documentary imagery over polished or synthetic stand-ins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text and controls. Keep Signal Lime on dark surfaces or in non-text accents, never as paragraph text on a light background. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, descriptive image alternatives, and readable line lengths.
